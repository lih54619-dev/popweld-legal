This Privacy Policy explains how Shandong Popweld Automatic Equipment Co.,Ltd ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects information when you contact us through our Facebook Page, our website, WhatsApp, email or other channels.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us — for example by sending a message to our Facebook Page, emailing us, or requesting a quotation — we may collect:

  • Contact details you provide: name, company name, country, email address, phone or WhatsApp number.
  • Message content: the text of messages you send us, and any files (drawings, specifications, photos) you choose to share.
  • Business information needed to prepare a quotation: the welding application, pipe or tank dimensions, material and quantity.
  • Basic technical data automatically provided by the platform (for example your Facebook profile ID when you message our Page), used only to reply to you and keep our conversation history.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To answer your enquiry and provide product information or quotations.
  • To process and deliver orders, and to provide after-sales and warranty service.
  • To keep a record of our business communication with you.
  • To improve our products and customer service.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

3. Facebook / Meta Platform Data

Our Facebook Page uses Meta's Messenger Platform so that we can receive and reply to messages you send us. When you message our Page, Meta provides us with your Page-scoped ID and the content of your message so that we can respond. We use this data only to communicate with you and to provide the products or information you requested.

We do not use Facebook messaging data for advertising, profiling or any purpose unrelated to answering your enquiry. Our use of Meta platform data complies with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

4. Sharing and Disclosure

We may share information only with:

  • Logistics and freight partners — to arrange shipment and customs clearance.
  • Service providers — such as our email or CRM provider, strictly to operate our business communication.
  • Authorities — where required by applicable law.

5. Data Retention

We keep enquiry and order records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal, accounting and warranty obligations. You may ask us to delete your data at any time (see section 7).

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Access is limited to staff who need it to serve you.

7. Your Rights — Access and Deletion

You may contact us at any time to:

  • ask what information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct it;
  • ask us to delete it.

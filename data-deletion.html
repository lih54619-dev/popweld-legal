Data Deletion Instructions

Shandong Popweld Automatic Equipment Co.,Ltd — www.popweld.com

If you have contacted us through our Facebook Page (Messenger) or by email and would like us to delete the personal data we hold about you, please follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to lih54619@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Data Deletion Request.
  3. Tell us which channel you used to contact us (Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, email) and, if you know it, your name or company name so we can locate your records.

What happens next

  • We confirm receipt of your request by email.
  • We delete the personal data associated with your enquiry or order from our systems within 30 days.
  • We reply by email to confirm the deletion.

Please note: we may need to retain a limited record where the law requires it (for example accounting records for completed orders). Where this applies, we will tell you.

Deleting Facebook app data

You can also remove our app's access to your Facebook account at any time from your Facebook settings: Settings & Privacy → Settings → Apps and Websites. Removing our app stops any further data being shared with us; you may still email us using the steps above to have existing records deleted.
